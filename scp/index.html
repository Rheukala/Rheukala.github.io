SCP Foundation Catalog is an offline reader for the SCP Wiki: every SCP file, tale and the main hubs, on your device, with search, bookmarks and narration. If something is not working, the answers below cover the common cases. If yours is not here, write to us.

Unofficial fan app. Not affiliated with the SCP Wiki.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an internet connection?

No. The whole archive is included in the app and works offline. When you are online, the app downloads new and updated files in the background, and shows images from the wiki. You can save every image for offline reading in Display → Images for offline; the download is large (about 4 GB), so use Wi-Fi.

A file is missing or out of date

The archive is updated every week from the SCP Wiki, and the app picks up changes the next time it is online. A few numbers are not included because the wiki staff removed those pages; links to them say so. If a file you expect is missing, tell us its number or link.

Why do some images say "Image on the wiki"?

We only include images whose license clearly allows it (Creative Commons or public domain), and credit each one. Other images stay on the wiki; tap View on the wiki to see them there.

Why can't I reveal the black bars?

Most redactions in SCP files are part of the story: the original page shows black bars with nothing behind them, and the app shows them the same way. Where an author did hide text under a redaction, the bar has an outline and you can tap it to reveal the text.

Some parts of a file say "only on the wiki"

A small number of pages use videos or interactive elements that cannot work offline. Tap Open on the wiki to use them there. Most interactive parts of pages do work inside the app.

How does narration work?

Tap Narration at the bottom of a file. Reading continues with the screen locked, and you can pause or skip paragraphs from the lock screen. Choose a voice and speed in Display. For the most natural voices, download one in iOS Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → English (Enhanced and Premium voices are free).

Can I change the text size or theme?

Yes. Tap Aa for text size and a sans or serif reading font. The sun and moon switch chooses light or dark; Auto follows your device. A terminal theme is in Display.

Who wrote the stories?

The authors of the SCP Wiki. Every file shows its title, its authors, a link to the original page and its license. Does the app collect my data?

No. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or trackers. Details are in the privacy policy.

Privacy Policy

SCP Foundation Catalog · Developer: Rheukala · Last updated: November 11, 2026

SupportContact

In short: SCP Foundation Catalog does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or trackers.

What stays on your device

Your settings (theme, text size, voice), bookmarks, reading history and reading progress, the archive itself and any images you save for offline reading. They are stored only on your device and never sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes them.

What the app downloads

FromWhatWhy
rheukala.github.ioPublic content files (new and updated SCP files)To keep the archive current
SCP Wiki file hosts (wikidot.com, wdfiles.com)Freely licensed images shown in filesTo display images
Wikimedia Commons (upload.wikimedia.org)Freely licensed images used by some filesTo display images

These are ordinary web requests for public files. They carry no account, identifier or personal information. Like any website, these servers may record standard technical information such as an IP address in their logs; we do not receive or use it.

Links

Links to the SCP Wiki and other websites open in Safari. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Narration

Narration uses the speech voices built into iOS. The text is spoken on your device and is not sent anywhere.

Children

The app is rated 17+ for horror content. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

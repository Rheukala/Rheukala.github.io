Privacy Policy

Tide: Sea Glass Puzzle · Developer: Rheukala · Last updated: November 10, 2026

SupportContact

Tide is a puzzle game. It has no accounts, no sign-in and no social features. This page explains the small amount of information the app handles, which companies receive it and why, and how to reach us. It is written to match what the app actually does.

In short

  • Tide never asks for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • Your progress is saved on your device. We do not keep a copy.
  • The app uses Google's Firebase for anonymous usage statistics and crash reports, Google AdMob for optional rewarded ads shown without personalization, and Apple and RevenueCat for in-app purchases.
  • We do not sell personal information, and we do not use your data to track you across other apps or websites.

1. Data kept on your device

Tide stores the following in the app's own storage on your device so the game remembers you between sessions: levels cleared and stars, Build Stars and restored scenes, pearls and tools, daily puzzle results, your settings (sound, theme, larger text, reduced motion) and a random install identifier created on your device. This data stays on the device. Deleting the app deletes all of it. You can also erase it from inside the app with Settings → Reset progress.

When you tap Share on a daily puzzle result, the app builds a short text card and hands it to your device's share sheet. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose where to share it.

2. Usage statistics and crash reports (Firebase)

To see which levels are too hard, where players get stuck and whether the app crashes, Tide uses Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. They receive:

  • gameplay events such as "level started", "level won" or "tool used", with the level number and app version;
  • a random, anonymous app-instance identifier generated by Firebase for this installation;
  • device model, operating system version, language, country (approximated from your IP address) and session timing;
  • crash reports: the state of the app when it crashed, the device model and OS version.

These events contain no names, email addresses or other personal details. Firebase Remote Config may also fetch a few tuning numbers from Google's servers at start-up; this sends no data about you. Google's handling of this data is described in its privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy and in firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

3. Ads (Google AdMob)

Tide shows rewarded ads only. An ad plays only when you tap an offer such as "watch an ad for +3 moves", and there is a daily limit. There are no banner ads and no ads between levels.

Ads are served by Google AdMob and are requested as non-personalized. That means ads are chosen by context, not by a profile of you, and the app does not ask for permission to track you on iOS. To serve and measure an ad, Google still receives technical information: device type and operating system, your IP address and the approximate location derived from it, device identifiers used for frequency capping and fraud prevention, and whether the ad was viewed or tapped. See policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

You can limit ad tracking in your device settings (on iPhone: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking and Apple Advertising).

4. In-app purchases (Apple and RevenueCat)

Pearl packs and the optional Gemstone theme are sold through the Apple App Store. Apple handles payment; Tide never sees your card details. To confirm purchases and restore them on a new device, Tide uses RevenueCat, which receives the purchase receipt, the product bought, an anonymous app user ID and basic device and app information. See revenuecat.com/privacy and apple.com/legal/privacy.

5. Children

Tide is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are always requested without personalization. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your data: uninstalling the app, or using Settings → Reset progress, removes everything stored on your device. To ask us to delete the anonymous analytics or crash data tied to your installation, email us with the app version and roughly when you played; we will request deletion from the providers above.
  • Ads: you can simply never tap an ad offer. The game never requires one.
  • Purchases: refunds are handled by Apple through reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Depending on where you live (for example the EU and UK under the GDPR, or California under the CCPA/CPRA) you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of personal data, and to complain to a supervisory authority. Contact us to exercise these rights. We will not treat you differently for doing so.

7. Security and retention

Data on your device is protected by your device's own security. Data held by Google, Apple and RevenueCat is kept according to their policies; Firebase Analytics data is retained for a limited period set in our project configuration, and we keep it only as long as it is useful for improving the game.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new "Last updated" date. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.

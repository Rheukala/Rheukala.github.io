Tide is a calm puzzle about water and glass: tap groups of matching sea glass to pop them, let the current carry the rest into place, and restore the coast one Build Star at a time. If something is not working, the answers below cover the common cases. If yours is not here, write to us.

Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Tap any group of two or more touching pieces of the same color to pop it. The current then carries the remaining pieces to fill the gaps, so each board flows in its own direction. Clear the goals shown at the top before you run out of moves. Groups of five or more make a Wave, and seven or more make a Whirl. Press and hold a piece to preview what it will do.

I lost my progress

Progress is saved on your device, and the app keeps a backup copy that it restores automatically if the main save is damaged. Progress is lost if the app is deleted, or if Settings → Reset progress is used. There is no account, so we cannot restore progress from our side. Purchases can still be restored (see below).

I bought pearls or the Gemstone theme but did not receive them

Pearls are added the moment Apple confirms the purchase. If they do not appear, close the app fully and open it again while connected to the internet. If they are still missing, send us the date of the purchase and the receipt email from Apple, and we will put it right. The Gemstone theme is tied to your Apple ID, so it comes back on a new device once you are signed in with the same account. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

An ad offer is not showing, or an ad did not give the reward

Ads in Tide are optional and rewarded only. Offers are limited to a few per day, need a few seconds between them, and need an internet connection. If an ad fails to load, the offer hides itself rather than charging you. If you watched an ad to the end and did not get the reward, tell us which offer it was and when, and we will add it.

What is Today's Passage?

A new daily puzzle, the same board for everyone. It unlocks after the first few levels. Finish it in as few moves as you can, then tap Share to send your result as a short text card. The next passage arrives at midnight, local time.

What are Build Stars and pearls?

Every level you clear for the first time gives a Build Star. Spend them in the Harbor to restore it step by step, then move on to the next place on the coast. Pearls are the in-game currency for tools and extra moves. You earn them by clearing levels, finishing chapters, and from the daily gift; you can also buy them in the Shop.

Can I play offline?

Yes. The whole game is on your device. Only ad offers and purchases need a connection.

The board is hard to read

Every color has its own shape, so pieces can be told apart without relying on color. In Settings you can turn on Larger text, choose Reduce motion, or switch to Clear Glass once it is unlocked, which gives the pieces a brighter outline.

How do I turn off sound or music?

Tap the pause button during a level, or open Settings from the title screen. Sound effects and music can be set separately.

Does Tide collect my data?

Tide has no accounts and never asks for personal details. It uses anonymous usage statistics and crash reports to improve the game, and shows ads without personalization. The full detail is in the privacy policy.
